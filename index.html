History of Origami

Where Origami Began

Origami is the traditional Japanese art of paper folding. The word comes from the Japanese words "ori" (to fold) and "kami" (paper). What began centuries ago as a cultural practice has now become a creative activity enjoyed around the world.

From simple cranes to intricate animal designs, origami combines imagination, patience, and skill using only a single sheet of paper.
